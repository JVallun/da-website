Independent engineering advisory and review across the pressure-equipment, welding and process-safety lifecycle. Document-review services run on a high-throughput review system built for speed and accuracy together: large document sets turn around on short cycles and keep schedules moving; critical and major findings are adversarially verified before they reach you, each stating the code edition it assumes; and every finding carries its source citation — accuracy you can check at the source, not take on trust. What we do

2.01

Mechanical integrity & pressure equipment

API 510/570 inspection advisory, API 580 risk-based inspection programme support, PER/SANS 347 compliance review, inspection-plan and RBI verification checks.

2.02

Welding & materials QA/QC

WPS/PQR and welder-qualification review, fabrication surveillance, ISO 3834 readiness scans, materials and weldability assessments.

2.03

Independent NDT report review

UT/PAUT/TOFD, MT, PT and RT report packs checked against acceptance criteria, personnel certification, calibration and coverage claims.

2.04

Process safety & risk engineering

PSM/mechanical-integrity programme build-out, HAZOP/What-If/FMECA study support and readiness reviews, MHI 2022 compliance preparation (MIPP, safety reports, licence packs).

2.05

Facility & site safety assessments

structured walkdown inspections of buildings and operating facilities against a defined checklist, delivered as a findings report with the completed checklist as the evidence pack; multi-site corporate programmes supported.

2.06

Deterministic procedure assurance

2.08

2.09

2.10

Engineering document review at scale

03

How engagements run

Fixed-scope review packages (defined deliverable, lump-sum quote), day-rate advisory on- or off-site, or a monthly integrity-desk retainer. Every deliverable ships with its evidence pack — findings traced to their cited clause and source document, with the edition assumed stated.

Scope boundary, stated plainly: this is independent advisory, readiness and review work. Statutory AIA inspections and certification sign-offs remain with accredited inspection authorities — we scope, coordinate and package the evidence; the statutory instrument always belongs to the accreditation holder.
